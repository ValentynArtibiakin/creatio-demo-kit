# {{TITLE}}: how to use and edit it

Link: {{URL}}

This is the clickable walkthrough of {{DEMO}}: {{N_UC}} use cases, {{N_SCREENS}} screens. People it is shared with can open it and comment on it; with **Contributor (Can interact)** access they can also change it. Changes show for everyone at once; nothing has to be rebuilt.

## Find your way
- **Use-case buttons** at the top jump to the first screen of that story.
- **Prev / Next**, the **strip of screens** under the screen, or the ← → keys.
- Inside a screen, the elements the presenter clicks go to the next screen. Tick **Show clickable** to outline them, and **Build labels** to show the OOTB / Config / New / Custom / Demo mock labels. Both are off by default.
- Above each screen: **Do** (what the presenter clicks or types) and **What happens**. **Attention** (amber) is a risk at that step, **Issue** (red) a gap or blocker, and **Say** (collapsed) the talk track.
- **Gaps & blockers: N** walks through every screen with an open issue. The same issues are marked on the screens with a red BLOCKER or amber GAP badge.

## Change a screen with Claude
1. Open the screen and click **Change with Claude** (bottom right).
2. Type or say what should change, for example "show the amount as 9,500" or "add a Warranty tab next to Timeline". The microphone takes voice; **EN / UA** switches its language. If the microphone is blocked, use keyboard dictation (Win+H on Windows, Fn twice on a Mac).
3. Claude runs on your own Claude account. It changes the screen and, when needed, the step text above it. The change is **saved for everyone as soon as it appears**.
4. Not happy? **Undo last saved change** brings the previous version back. **Versions** lists the earlier versions of the screen; **Restore** brings any of them back (the replaced one is kept too). If saving fails, an orange bar above the screen says why, with **Retry saving** and **Discard**.

With view or comment access, Claude's change stays on your screen only and the page says so; send it to the team with **💬 Comment** instead.

## Edit the step text by hand
**Edit step text** (top right, Contributor access): each step has Who, Do, What happens, Say, Attention and Issue. Add or remove steps, then **Save for everyone**; **Done editing** goes back. Clearing the Issue of a step takes it off the Gaps & blockers counter.

## Comment
**💬 Comment**, then click a field, a table row, a KPI or a step block, and write what is wrong, missing, or how you would show it.

## What is not on the stand
The screens are a prototype drawn from the demo script. Numbers, names and AI answers the script does not give are sample values. Ask the owner of the walkthrough for Contributor access if you cannot save.
